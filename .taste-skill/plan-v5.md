# Ambitio PhD dashboard — scenic scroll version

Kind: software. User-supplied 24-second Cora recording is motion and composition reference only.

## Direction

Use one typeface: Geist. Give the product a continuous, open-air story without recreating Cora's email metaphor. A cloud opening leads to a white decision chapter, a blue finder showcase, and an original golden landscape representing the path ahead. Keep Ambitio's real dashboard captures readable, full-ratio, and separate from editorial visuals.

## Scroll and interaction

- Hero: clouds drift slowly; headline recedes; full dashboard rises, always retaining its complete aspect ratio.
- Challenge: four questions progress on one sticky stage. CSS diagrams connect research direction, supervisors, programmes, and funding without pretending to be dashboard UI.
- Finders: three scroll-linked product states replace one another with a short directional transition; no autoplay.
- Programme detail: one large real screenshot enters as the section becomes visible.
- AI/profile: original landscape background carries two complete dashboard captures as separate cards.
- Planning: selectable Shortlist Builder and Interview Prep, with visible Elite gate note.
- Reduced motion: static readable content and no scroll-linked transforms.
