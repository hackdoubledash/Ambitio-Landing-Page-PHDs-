# Ambitio dashboard landing page — research and V1 story

Status: planning draft, 26 November 2026. No page design or implementation is approved by this document. The page is a **single landing page** for Ambitio's dashboard, with a full footer. The live dashboard currently looks PhD focused; confirm whether this paid page targets only PhD applicants before final copy.

## Evidence and boundaries

- **Observed in the signed-in dashboard:** [nine screenshots](dashboard-screens/) covering Home, PhD Finder results and programme detail, Supervisor Finder, Funding Finder, Lisa chat, Research Profile Audit, Shortlist Builder gate, and Interview Prep gate.
- **Observed on [Ambitio's public site](https://ambitio.club/):** Ambitio positions itself around student-first, data-backed admissions guidance. Its current plans include Apex for PhD applicants. The public footer has product and legal links.
- **User-provided visual references:** a bright blue sky and floating-card hero, a violet dashboard-led SaaS page, and a blue/white product page with features, pricing, FAQs and a closing CTA. These establish visual ambition, not product facts.
- **Unknown:** whether this is a standalone dashboard sale or a lead-in to counselling; price and plan structure; which gated tools this landing page can promise; destination for the primary CTA; approved testimonials and numerical claims.

## 3. Copy and story principles

The research points to a simple sequence: name a student's task, show the product doing it, then answer the next question. [A 2025 Medium article on story-led landing pages](https://medium.com/@aigerim_tulekova/landing-pages-that-read-like-stories-and-convert-like-sales-3a0d008f5d5d) proposes a problem → possibility → product → proof → next-step arc. [A 2026 SaaS landing-page article](https://medium.com/@pasha2do/saas-landing-page-structure-that-converts-in-2026-f534f2d76d95) reinforces plain headlines, real product visuals and a focused CTA. Treat these as editorial guidance, not verified conversion results.

**Proposed story:** “A PhD search involves more than choosing a university. See the programmes, people and funding around your research direction; understand where your profile stands; then plan the next steps in one place.”

This story leaves room for a polished cloud visual: the visual can express possibility and discovery while the copy and screenshots communicate actual product value. Motion should clarify transitions between tasks, not hide the interface.

## 4. Draft copy, version A

Draft assumes the audience is **prospective PhD applicants**. All copy below is proposed, not approved Ambitio messaging. Avoid implying guaranteed admission, guaranteed funding, or access to gated tools with every plan.

### Hero

- Eyebrow: `The PhD journey, in one place`
- Headline: `Find the right PhD path for your research.`
- Supporting line: `Explore programmes, supervisors and funding opportunities. Understand your profile and plan what comes next with Ambitio.`
- Primary CTA: `Explore the dashboard` **[destination to confirm]**
- Secondary CTA: `See how it works` **[on-page anchor]**
- Visual: genuine Home screen, with Finder cards in a cloud atmosphere. The visual can be composed for the page, but the UI itself should remain recognizable and honest.

### Transition / problem

- Heading: `A PhD search has a lot of moving parts.`
- Body: `The right programme has to fit your research, the right supervisor has to work in your field, and the funding has to make the move possible. Ambitio brings those decisions into one journey.`

### Discover group

- Section heading: `Explore the opportunities around your research.`
- Programme card: `Find programmes that fit your direction.` / `Search by course, country, PhD type and start date, then open the details that matter before you shortlist.`
- Supervisor card: `Find researchers in your field.` / `Explore faculty by university, department and research focus.`
- Funding card: `See funding possibilities alongside your search.` / `Browse scholarships and funding opportunities by type, discipline, country and deadline.`

### AI / profile group

- Section heading: `Get clearer on where you stand.`
- Audit: `Start with your research profile.` / `The Research Profile Audit is designed to review your research direction, alignment and funding viability, then surface areas to strengthen.` **[wording should be checked against a completed audit]**
- Lisa: `Ask Lisa as questions come up.` / `Use Ambitio's in-dashboard assistant to explore study-abroad questions while you plan.`

### Planning / gated group

- Heading: `Turn discovery into a plan.`
- Body: `Bring promising programmes, professors and positions into a shortlist, then prepare for the steps ahead.`
- Availability note: `Shortlist Builder and interview-preparation tools appear behind Ambitio Elite in the current dashboard.` **[confirm whether to include/promote on this separate product page]**

### Proof / FAQ / close

- Proof heading: `See the journey, not just the promise.` Use a verified product walkthrough or approved student story; do not invent testimonials, outcomes or metrics.
- FAQ candidates: `Who is this dashboard for?` `What can I explore before upgrading?` `How does the Research Profile Audit work?` `Which planning tools are part of Elite?` Answers need product-owner confirmation.
- Closing heading: `Start with a clearer picture of your PhD options.`
- Closing CTA: `Explore the dashboard` **[destination to confirm]**

## 5. Single-page sitemap and section order

This is a **page structure**, not separate website routes.

| Order | Section | Job in the story | Suggested evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Header | Identity, a few anchor links, primary CTA | Ambitio mark; `Explore`, `AI & profile`, `How it works`, `FAQs` |
| 2 | Hero | Audience + core promise + real product | Home dashboard and selected Finder fragments inside cloud treatment |
| 3 | Problem / journey | Show why these decisions belong together | Compact programme / people / funding visual, no invented statistics |
| 4 | Discover | Explain programme, supervisor and funding search | Finder screenshots, possibly selector interaction |
| 5 | Programme detail | Show depth beyond a results list | Programme-detail tabs, especially research focus, eligibility, funding and supervisors |
| 6 | Profile + Lisa | Show AI in actual use cases | Audit entry screen and Lisa chat, clearly distinguished |
| 7 | Plan / prepare | Connect search to shortlist and applications | Gated shortlist and interview prep, availability caveat until scope clarified |
| 8 | How it works | Give a short path: explore → understand → plan | Three concise steps built from the observed workflow |
| 9 | Proof | Earn trust with an approved story or real walkthrough | Placeholder until verified material supplied |
| 10 | FAQs | Resolve access, eligibility, pricing and tool questions | Questions drafted; answers pending brief |
| 11 | Closing CTA | One clear next action | CTA destination pending |
| 12 | Footer | Product and company navigation, social and legal | Ambitio's live footer as the source of labels and links |

The page can be shorter if the brief narrows the offer. Specifically, `Programme detail` could merge into `Discover`, and `How it works` could merge into `Plan / prepare` without losing the story.
